1. The assignment became a measurement problem before it became a modeling problem.
2. Odometry and simulation were not enough; I needed an external final-pose measurement.
3. Repeatable starts became a necessary control variable.
4. The same command interface hid different stop conditions between simulation and real hardware.
5. I chose a small empirical probabilistic model because the task asked for a rough endpoint prediction.
6. The final route validation exposed the limit of that model.
7. The takeaway is exactly the motivation for SLAM: without continuous localization, endpoint prediction is brittle.
