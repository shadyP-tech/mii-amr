**1. Highest priority: make LiDAR → camera motion handoff reliable**

The executing listener recorded eleven missing `map ← odom` lookups, then a first available transform aged **1.664346 s**, exceeding the **1.0 s** freshness limit. It stopped at **3.065874 s** of a **5 s** startup budget. Meanwhile `odom ← base_footprint` was fresh on all twelve attempts, scan/odometry were fresh, and the isolated TF executor was alive. The stop was appropriate for using that sample; a generic “TF unavailable” message hid the more useful stale-sample diagnosis. [Recorded failure](/Users/stephpark/Documents/stephsWorld/mii-amr/results/audits/stand_explore_exact2_camera_20260909T123820Z/results/aufgabe04/real/autonomous_exploration/stand_explore_exact2_camera_20260909T123820Z/mission_failure.json).

Preflight and the follower have different listeners. A preflight pass does not establish freshness in the newly created execution buffer. The bundle does not resolve whether the delayed global transform arose from publisher scheduling, DDS discovery/transport, or queued delivery. A healthy executor heartbeat proves callback servicing, not delivery of the required edge.

Current workspace changes already add a tightly bounded wait for a structurally valid first stale global sample, improved diagnostics, and consistent binding between human-readable and typed recovery reasons. These need a real repeat. The reason-binding correction alone does not fix the recorded stale-sample case. The existing [focused run audit](/Users/stephpark/Documents/stephsWorld/mii-amr/docs/setups/aufgabe04_run_audit_20260909T123820Z.md) documents the distinction.

Optimize the transition by measuring per-edge source stamps, callback receipt times, first-fresh-sample time, executor delay and the interval from preflight to follower readiness. Check delivery in the actual executing process. Keep the existing deadline and require a fresh, valid transform plus route/localization admission before motion. Consider retaining listener lifetime across routine legs only if measurements show process startup dominates; keep one motion owner and per-leg certification.

AMCL in Humble republishes its last valid transform on processed scans even when no filter update is needed, and timestamps it using scan time plus transform tolerance. Therefore “the robot was stationary” alone does not explain stale global TF. Inspect actual scan processing/delivery and clocks before changing AMCL thresholds. [Humble AMCL source](https://api.nav2.org/nav2-humble/html/amcl__node_8cpp_source.html).

Proposed next validation: ten repeated unloaded coverage-to-candidate transitions, logging every attempt. Exercise both missing→fresh and missing→old→fresh startup sequences offline; persistent stale, malformed/future TF and established-edge loss must stop. Hardware acceptance requires reaching camera observation with fresh execution inputs, not merely passing preflight.

**2. Camera priority: fit geometry and timing into the same observation budget**

The September 8 evidence shows two distinct problems: border/QR geometry disagreement and obsolete results. In the 16:33 clip, four joint QR/head fits failed, five frames lacked sufficient corner evidence, and two lacked corridor refinement. Tracked misses displayed 823–913 ms processing. A one-frame offline probe attributed 521 ms of a 548 ms QR call to fallback decoding; disabling that fallback yielded 26.6 ms. These are diagnostic/replay timings, not a current real-observer benchmark. [Evidence and limits](/Users/stephpark/Documents/stephsWorld/mii-amr/results/aufgabe04/debug_audits/recording_20260908_163300_798534660/audit.md:44).

Several remedies are already in current code: verified-only tracker updates, absolute observation-age checks, bounded QR work on tracked frames, refined-pose overlays and better residual/stage diagnostics. The [stored validation](/Users/stephpark/Documents/stephsWorld/mii-amr/results/aufgabe04/debug_audits/modular_stand_detection_fix_20260908/validation.json) reports 149 focused passes; six broader failures were reproduced at the unchanged baseline. It explicitly provides no new robot recording validating angle accuracy. Do not reimplement those fixes or count them as hardware success.

There is a remaining timing-contract risk. The tracker only admits/reuses poses within **250 ms** of observation. September 9 pre-motion timing recorded image age **252.9 ms** before vision; its nineteen image headers had receipt-minus-stamp ages of **114.5 / 265.4 / 396.5 ms** minimum/median/maximum, with twelve already over 250 ms. A timing preflight can therefore pass while the tracking fast path is unlikely to remain usable. This is a measured input-timing risk, not proof of tracker starvation during that run: the camera observer never started. [Timing evidence](/Users/stephpark/Documents/stephsWorld/mii-amr/results/audits/stand_explore_exact2_camera_20260909T123820Z/results/aufgabe04/real/autonomous_exploration/stand_explore_exact2_camera_20260909T123820Z/preflight/stand_explore_exact2_camera_20260909T123820Z_candidate_000_camera_lidar_timing_before_motion.json:230), [tracker contract](/Users/stephpark/Documents/stephsWorld/mii-amr/scripts/aufgabe04/perception/stand_axis/pose_tracking.py:41).

Measure capture→receipt→processing-start→completion and the age at the next prediction. Reduce transport/queue age and repeated decoder calls; share the same decoded text-and-corners observation across registration, geometry and identity logic. Budget full acquisition separately from tracked geometry. Optimize for fresh accepted target observations per second and time to a validated QR-facing pose. Increasing expiry alone does not reduce observation error.

Use original images, CameraInfo and measured camera/stand geometry to distinguish the **78 mm physical head**, **71 mm paper panel**, and **62 mm QR symbol**. A low head-only residual can still fit the wrong nested border. Preserve semantic corner checks and report head/QR residuals separately; calibrate exposure/focus/lighting and stand-off distance against QR readability and pose error.

The debug viewer's explicit camera/LiDAR angular handoff and full face-normal transform are not the production observer path. Production performs LiDAR target bearing/range association and derives its map axis from camera yaw/heading. This is a validation/integration gap, not proof that its output is numerically wrong. Test known front, oblique and back views across robot headings and camera pitch before adopting shared full-normal transformation and angular-consistency checks.

Proposed camera validation: a stationary front/oblique/back/edge-on matrix at measured distances, with raw synchronized inputs and known stand orientation. Record angle bias/jitter, correct-border rate, stale-result rate, wrong-target/duplicate-QR rejection, tracker reuse and p50/p95 stage latency. Then demonstrate one candidate's complete approach→stop→QR-facing receipt→reapproach/opposite-face path before another five-candidate mission.

The production acquisition path can run full QR decoding during nominal registration, a widened proposal and strict retry on the same image. Share decoder work while preserving crop-coordinate and symbol identity binding. Also preserve failed frames in a bounded production-observer capture: the viewer's new raw recording support does not itself change the observer's overwriting of latest debug images. [Registration stages](/Users/stephpark/Documents/stephsWorld/mii-amr/scripts/aufgabe04/real_robot/observer/camera_target_registration.py:208), [observer debug output](/Users/stephpark/Documents/stephsWorld/mii-amr/scripts/aufgabe04/real_robot/observer/node.py:1838).

**3. LiDAR: improve evidence quality and viewpoint choice**

The September 9 stops processed **79 + 75 scans** and accumulated **455 + 477 accepted observations**. Temporal tracks were reduced by morphology and static-map filters, then fused into five hypotheses. That is meaningful progress. Repeated detections in one stationary epoch are not independent viewpoints, and five hypotheses do not prove five correctly located physical stations.

The reported **95.31% coverage** is computed from visited planned-view coverage cells. It is not measured proof that 95.31% of stand surfaces were visible. Three candidates remain single-view, and two terminal visibility-eligibility advisories remain unresolved. Use actual ray visibility and candidate uncertainty to decide useful follow-up observations. Preserve the exact-two experiment as its defined checkpoint; use a separately specified adaptive exploration policy when additional observations are allowed.

The real scan is approximately **1.61° per ray**, with roughly 221–226 rays in recorded receipts. Some detector commentary/tests assume roughly 1°/360 rays, although the implementation correctly uses the supplied angle increment. At 1.35 m, 1.61° corresponds to about **38 mm transverse beam spacing**. Measure the actual stand cross-section at laser height, range, incidence angle and missing-return pattern before tuning minimum points, width, confidence or visibility radius. The camera head width is not automatically the LiDAR-visible width.

One concrete robustness defect was reproduced without ROS: two adjacent 1.9 m returns pass as a candidate away from the scan seam, but equivalent returns split between indices 359 and 0 fail. [Clustering](/Users/stephpark/Documents/stephsWorld/mii-amr/scripts/aufgabe04/perception/lidar_stand_detector.py:48) never joins first/last clusters and [minimum-points filtering](/Users/stephpark/Documents/stephsWorld/mii-amr/scripts/aufgabe04/perception/lidar_stand_detector.py:88) rejects the resulting singletons. Add circular seam handling only for a genuinely full-circle scan, with wrap, dropout and partial-FOV tests. This is not established as a cause of the recorded September failure.

Proposed LiDAR validation: measured stand positions across range/incidence and the scan seam, including walls/radiator clutter. Report recall, false candidates, centre error, duplicate merges/splits and useful independent view count. Rank follow-up views by expected resolution of provisional candidates, route cost and camera feasibility. Keep unresolved hypotheses in route keepouts until evidence supports removal.

The morphology profile's expected 0.12 m diameter currently comes from a navigation candidate-radius proxy, explicitly not stand metrology. Replace that assumption with a measured laser-plane shape/range/incidence model after collecting labeled data. [Profile derivation](/Users/stephpark/Documents/stephsWorld/mii-amr/scripts/aufgabe04/perception/lidar_stand_morphology.py:270). Add received-scan, exact-TF-timeout, queue/drop and latency counts to observer summaries; processed-scan totals alone omit rejected input.
