Candidate-guided neutral acquisition can run before QR or a successful pose exists. Viewer QR/tracker projections can locate a search region, but every accepted angle is recomputed from current borders without using historical pose to resolve ambiguity. Legacy backside search may supply another current proposal; its prior angle and side label cannot bypass the new quality checks. Explicit head-fit failure does not fall through to a more permissive joint fit.

The model uses the measured **78 mm head**; QR symbol dimensions are unchanged. Fitting the planar head's four measured corners supplies its 3D camera pose. The head depth and stem in the dashed purple overlay remain projected model geometry, not additional independently measured landmarks. The overlay design is preserved. Status text now reports current head yaw and conditional pixel-model uncertainty, and labels obsolete results explicitly instead of leaving their model status green.

Border selection does not rank hypotheses by their resulting angle or pose residual. When a supported rectangle has a detached neck, recovery uses its measured current gap to create at most two expanded search seeds. All four borders, corner arms and the junction must exist in the original edge image; missing outer edges are never drawn into the evidence. Original and recovered corner sets remain separately recorded. Tests include a measured paper rectangle with the true neck but no outer head rails, and a scene where both rectangles exist and recovery must return the actual outer corners and depth.

The initial quality limits require a shortest observed edge of **24 px**, raw-border support of at least **0.60**, complete accepted corner support, centered neck evidence with at most **2 missing pixel rows** below the head, reprojection RMSE at most **2 px**, positive depth for every corner, and no competing axis within the pixel-noise ambiguity allowance. A scaled full-rank projection Jacobian supplies a local yaw standard deviation using a pixel-noise floor of **0.75 px**; admission requires at most **3°**. This is an explicit engineering uncertainty model, not calibrated physical angle accuracy. Edge-on, very small and weakly observable near-frontal views can still be rejected. Increasing an angle limit cannot fix those cases.

The junction allowance tightens for small heads. The smaller vertical side span and the measured **78/71 mm head/paper** dimensions estimate the visible panel inset. The admitted integer gap must be below that inset minus a **2 px** edge-localization/quantization allowance, and never exceed 2 px. An unresolved inset is rejected. This closes the reproduced paper-only case at 0.9 m without using QR-symbol dimensions or decoded corners. These resolution assumptions still require physical validation; this is not an absolute boundary-identity proof.

An accepted head plane alone leaves front/back unresolved. It does not certify a backside because QR decoding failed. The autonomous observer requires an independently associated current QR marker/payload matching its temporal identity evidence before committing a facing recommendation. Unresolved new-source head consensus remains advisory. This removes the old physical pipeline's ability to produce an opposite-side receipt solely from head geometry plus repeated QR absence; a separate positively validated side detector is required to restore that behavior safely. The standalone legacy classifier remains available for compatibility tests.

The viewer also suppresses color-only backside classification and `basic_color_side` observation exports for the new source. Its optional legacy QR observation export still uses bounded-age background decoding and is not equivalent to the autonomous observer's current own-symbol binding and mission receipt.

Freshness, synchronized sensor tuples, stopped observation, final LiDAR association, conflict rejection, sample count and motion admission remain required. No physical dimensions, route-clearance limits or robot-motion permissions are broadened. Immutable recommendation provenance records the actual axis source and quality instead of labeling every real observation as the old joint estimator.
